## Content decision and implementation

All 76 location pages already had distinct English and Chinese enquiry briefs, repair decisions and option comparisons. The short repair decisions on twelve existing areas lacked enough explanation to distinguish the cause, retained parts and complete quoted outcome. They are expanded with individual guidance:

- Redfern: matching trim profiles, joint locations, wall condition and finish boundaries.
- Rockdale: runner compatibility, paired replacement, fixing substrates and final travel.
- Brookvale: paling counts, rail/post condition, access and appearance of retained/new boards.
- Kogarah: hinge, floor and moisture distinctions before cutting a bathroom door.
- Gordon: fascia/gutter trade coordination, sound fixing points and concealed damage.
- Macquarie Park: torn cabinet fixing points, compatible hardware and adjoining doors.
- Manly: opening performance, timber repair, coating, mechanisms and usable rooms.
- Kellyville: wide-gate weight, joints, post work, practical clearance and pool-gate scope.
- Surry Hills: paint build-up versus movement, retained clearance and rated-door identity.
- Neutral Bay: shelf span, brackets, intended use, substrate and making good.
- Mosman: gate sag versus post movement, alignment and closing checks.
- Castle Hill: numbered fence spans, retained components, staged repairs and shared access.

These are assessment scenarios, not claims about local housing stock or jobs in a suburb. No invented case, price, licence, review or branch is added. The original 56 enquiry briefs retain their verified fingerprint. All existing title/H1/route owners remain unchanged.

On all 152 location routes, the individual repair decision and comparison now precede the shared service gallery. The gallery retains all eleven illustrated choices, images and service links, but omits the eleven repeated service introductions. Full service answers remain on their existing owner pages. This makes the individual decision easier to reach while preserving all original photographic entrances.
