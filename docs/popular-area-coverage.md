The screenshot's UI labels (including the unrelated Hobart heading, unlimited option and more-regions control) are not service locations. Sydney City uses the existing Sydney CBD route rather than creating a second intent owner.

## Editorial boundaries

- Each addition has its own enquiry situation, scope decision, repair comparison and relevant service selection. These are conditional preparation guides, not unsupported claims about local housing or damage prevalence.
- No job locality, before/after outcome, travel price, attendance promise or local office is invented. Existing real galleries remain service evidence without being relabelled as work in these locations.
- Availability, access, scope and any travel charges are discussed before booking. These named locations do not imply service throughout NSW.
- The popular-location directory, regional directory, cross-links, maps and editable RFQ provide discovery and enquiry routes. All intended public URLs have self-canonicals and reciprocal language links and are in the sitemap; this does not guarantee Google indexing or rankings.
